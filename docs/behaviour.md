# How the buddy behaves

Everything the buddy does in the game: the signs you give them, what they do on their own, the jobs you switch on with a tap, mob farms, how they find their way, how they choose what to do, and what they carry.

## Signs you give the buddy in the game

All of it is done by being near them; nothing needs a command or a particular server.

| You | The buddy |
|---|---|
| look at them, crouch twice quickly (empty hands, or anything that is no sword, spear, mace or trident: a pickaxe or an axe is fine) | stop and stay here / follow again (a nod or a hop); staying lasts over a restart |
| a sword, spear, mace or trident in hand, look at them, crouch twice | protection on (weapon out and a swing) or off (weapon away and a shake of the head) |
| a sword, spear, mace or trident held up to the sky, crouch twice | fighting players on or off (on by default, kept) |
| empty hands, look at them, crouch five times or more | "forget everything of this server?": they shake their head and crouch once; crouch twice within seven seconds to confirm, they nod |
| **tap a block with a bare hand** (hit it and let go within a second), with the buddy within six blocks | a job on or off (see below); they answer |
| **punch a monster in the pen of a mob farm with your bare hand**, the buddy following you and within six blocks | they farm it (see [Farming mobs](#farming-mobs)): a nod, then they walk to its edge, kill what comes in, walk round it and wait when it is empty |
| while they farm: crouch twice within six blocks of them (no need to look at them) | they stop farming: one crouch, they step back to where the pen's mobs cannot see them and wait there (in a base they keep the base) |
| throw things at them (Q while looking at them) | they take the things home to your base |

Their answers: **a nod** is yes or on, **one crouch** is off, **a shake of the head** is "that block means nothing to
me" or "that cannot be done", and with their weapon put away first: protection off.

## What the buddy does on their own

**Always:** they wait for the follow sign, and they are your **corpse guard**: when you die near them (within 32 blocks)
they fetch exactly what fell, the dearest first, and throw it at your feet when you are back. Not from lava, fire or
the void, not while more than two hostile mobs or a creeper stand by the spot or they are low themselves (they wait at a
distance while the items last), and not when another player killed you.

**While they follow you:** they follow (walking, swimming, climbing, jumping gaps, by elytra, on your happy ghast or in
your boat), guard you and themselves (a monster that hits them they hit back at once, also while they walk to a spot, get
loose or have a chest open; one they cannot get at for twenty seconds they leave alone a minute), sleep when you sleep, eat, respawn after a death, use `/tpa` and accept your `/tpahere`
where the server has them, learn your chests from the lids you open, wear their best armour, hold a **totem** in their
off hand whenever they have one, and a **shield** there while they guard you and have no totem,
and listen for your taps. **In a fight** they raise the shield between their blows, while a skeleton or anyone else aims
a bow or crossbow at them, and towards a creeper that swells within four blocks (its blast is blocked from the front);
at four hearts or less they back off and eat a golden apple, or their best food when they are not full, and fight on; with
three or more monsters around them they no longer join in on one you hit. **Their blows**: a critical hit for one foe; a
plain blow on the ground when two or more monsters stand beside it and the buddy's sword has Sweeping Edge (it sweeps the others
too); a sprinting blow at a creeper, which knocks it back, and then a step away; and a step back from a zombie or the
like that comes closer than a blow's length, where it is safe behind them. **The bow**: a phantom, a ghast, a blaze, a vex,
a creeper, a foe two and a half blocks or more above them, or one they cannot reach for four seconds, they shoot at with a
bow when they have one and arrows: standing, a full draw, aimed where the arrow meets it, never with a block or a player in
the arrow's way. **Ahead of a blow**: a monster that comes at you within six blocks (walking your way, set on a
target, closer than three blocks), a creeper that swells within seven of you and one that aims a bow at you, they take
on before it hits you, the worst first; with a monster within twelve blocks of you they stand beside you on its side,
facing it. Not an enderman, a piglin or a zombified piglin (a blow turns them on you), not a spider where it is light,
nor a mob with a name, on a lead, in a boat or a minecart, or in a pit or a farm's cell (an iron farm's zombie); not
while you play in creative or spectator mode, nor while they stay. **Players**: with **Guard against players** on (the
panel's follow card, on by default and kept over a restart), the buddy fights a player who hits you or them, and a player you hit,
as they would a monster, with an axe for a blow that takes a raised shield away; never you, a player you trust, or one in
creative or spectator mode. Against a player the buddy comes in at a run and strikes the first blow sprinting (it knocks the player
back), hits from 2.9 blocks, keeps two blocks off, strafes left and right while the strength of the next blow comes back, eats a golden
apple at once without walking off first (a player would catch up), and turns on whoever hits you, then on the weaker
of two who hit them. Golden apples they eat only to heal in a fight,
never for hunger. **Eating**: they always eat by themselves when they are hungry or low on hearts, from what they carry; not while you play
them with Take control, and again as soon as you stop. **Phantoms**: two game days after they last lay in a bed they lie down in the base's bed at night for a
second, also when nobody else sleeps (the server counts from that moment), and outside a base the panel says so. **At night in your base** they go to bed
there, also while they follow you, and get up when you leave the base or in the morning.

**Left in your base** (within 48 blocks of the bed you tapped, while they follow nobody or you told them to stay): they
**keep the base** for you: the fields that are on, the furnaces, the chests (everything they do not keep goes in),
and between jobs they stand by the bed; **at night they sleep** in the base's bed (or one beside it) until the morning.
With the setting **only when others sleep** (`baseSleepOthers`, off by default) they go to bed in the base only while
another player sleeps, from the first moment the server lets anyone sleep, and get up when nobody else has for ten
seconds. They see that from a player in bed within view,
or from the count of sleepers above the hotbar ("Slapen... (1/2)" on Tweakcraft, the game's own "1/2 players
sleeping" elsewhere), which reaches them wherever in the world the sleeper lies.
The base **grows** by itself: a chest or barrel near the bed that they can open becomes one of the base's; one they cannot
open is forgotten again. In the base they also do the **chores**:

- **compacting**: when the base's chests run full, they make blocks of what goes nine to a block (ingots, raw ore,
  diamonds, wheat into hay bales, ...) at a crafting table of the base, but leave a stack of what a furnace smelts or
  burns and half a stack of coal for their torches;
- **seeds** beyond a stack go into a composter, through a hopper on it (or a chest on that hopper) where there is one;
  a full composter they empty, and the bone meal goes into the chests;
- **sheep** of the base with wool are shorn (shears from a chest, or made from two iron ingots), from a cell beside
  the pen or inside it, never from its gate or doorway; they do not open a pen's gate while animals stand right behind
  it, and never leave one open; the setting **Shear sheep** (`baseShear`) switches it off;
- **strays**: a sheep or cow that got out of its pen is led back in with wheat, which they carry for it and hold only
  while they lead it; they shut the gate behind it from inside. The setting **Herd strays** (`baseHerd`) switches it off;
- **worn gear**: with the setting **Renew worn gear** (`baseRenew`, on) a tool or armour piece nearly worn out (a
  tenth of its durability left) is swapped for a fresh one from a chest, or a new iron one is made while the iron of
  **Leave iron** stays over. While they follow you away from the base, a pickaxe, axe, shovel or sword nearly worn out
  takes them home for it (the profile's home command, or on foot when the base is near) and back. They carry the new
  one and use the old one up first: of equally good tools they always take the most worn, to its last use; only a tool
  with an enchantment on it (no curse) is kept whole at three uses left, for a repair;
- **a shield and golden apples**: while they guard you, a shield of six planks and an iron ingot when they have none they
  can use and no chest holds one; golden apples of eight gold ingots and an apple while they and the chests have fewer
  than four. The settings **Leave iron** and **Leave gold** (`keepIron` 9 and `keepGold` 64, in ingots, a block
  counts nine) are what they never use of either, for anything they make;
- **a bow and arrows**: while they guard you, a bow of three sticks and three string when they have none and no chest holds
  one, and arrows (a flint, a stick and a feather make four) while they and the chests have fewer than 32;
- **tools**: a kind they lack and no chest holds is made at the crafting table, of iron while nine iron ingots stay
  over, else of copper while nine copper ingots stay over, else of stone (never of your diamonds); sticks and planks they
  make themselves, from planks or logs they fetch, and with no wood in the base they chop a tree of the base outside the
  garden around your chests (and plant its sapling);
- **armour**: a slot where they have nothing as good as iron, and no chest has such a piece, gets an iron piece made at
  the crafting table, while nine iron ingots stay over;
- **iron blocks** count as nine ingots each: when only blocks are left, they make one into ingots in their own grid.
- **storing**: in the base they look at all of its chests, not only those near them: what they carry goes into a chest
  that holds it, else into one where it lay before (they remember that per chest), and only then into the one with the
  most room;
- **trampled fields**: farmland of a known field that turned back into dirt (they saw it as farmland, or it has
  farmland on three sides) they hoe back and sow with what grows beside it; a hoe they lack is made, of iron while nine
  ingots stay over. They never land a jump on farmland, which is what tramples it.

From the chests they take food, tools, a weapon and armour by themselves, but nothing of diamond or netherite: those stay
for you.

## Jobs you switch on with a tap

Tap a block with your bare hand while the buddy stands near you. The same kind of block again switches it off. The jobs
stay as you left them over a restart (`assistDig`, `assistChop`, `assistFarm` in the settings).

| You tap | The buddy |
|---|---|
| farmland, or dirt or sand beside a crop or sugar cane | keeps this field: harvests and replants (a crop itself breaks at the first blow and sends no tap) |
| a log | chops along: the grown trees near you while you chop |
| stone, an ore, dirt, sand | digs along in the tunnel or pit you dig, and puts **torches** where it is dark (about ten blocks apart, on the right-hand wall of a tunnel; they fetch them from your chests or craft them from coal and sticks) |
| a furnace, blast furnace or smoker | **keeps it and the furnaces beside it going**: they open it and learn what is in it (to smelt on top, fuel below), then every minute take out what is done and fill it with more of what they learned, fetched from your chests |
| a chest or barrel | it is one of yours |
| a bed | **this is home**: they walk there and set their home with the server's plain `/sethome` |

On ground where you may not break (someone's claim, spawn) the server sends no tap; in creative a tap breaks the
block.

## Farming mobs

While the buddy follows you, **punch a monster in the pen of your mob farm with your bare hand**, standing within six blocks of
them. They look at where it stands: a closed space of at most 3 by 3 blocks that is a farm (a hopper, chest or hopper
minecart under it, a stream at its floor that carries the drops out of it, or mobs weakened by a fall, each by itself; a
shaft above it and a crowd of mobs only together), and a place at its edge to hit it from. Then **a nod**, and they go.
**A shake of the head**: it is closed but no farm (a small pool or a water hole with a mob in it, also one edged with
waterlogged stairs or roots, a cave nook), or there is nowhere to hit it from, or you or they stand in it (their chat says
which). **Open ground**: nothing, and your punch is a plain blow, which they help with at once when they guard you; a mob
that swims or flies with no floor within four blocks is open ground too, unless it drops down a shaft (then they wait for
it to land). At a pen they ignore the punch while they stay, are too far, ride something, are in AFK mode or another job of
theirs runs (the panel says why).

At the pen the buddy:

- walks to a spot at its edge, crouched as a player walks round a pit, and never over the pen; on an edge they stand
  crouched, so that they do not step off it (a blow from a mob on the land side can still knock them in);
- hits what comes in at a human pace, and only with the crosshair on it. With a sword and someone else (you, a pet, a
  villager) in reach of its sweep, they take an axe from their hotbar, which does not sweep, and their sword again a few
  seconds after you step away; with no axe in their hotbar they do not hit until you do;
- walks round to another side now and then (about every three minutes, `penSideSwitchSec`), and to another side for a
  mob they cannot reach from their own;
- waits and looks into the pen when it is empty.

**Danger:**

- Low on health after a blow (five hearts), they go where the pen's mobs cannot see them, eat, and come back at eight
  hearts or after a minute without damage. If they are hit there too, or keep being hit, they leave the farm and follow
  you; poison or fire that ticks on is no blow, and they wait that out there. When they have to back off three times in
  ten minutes, they leave the farm and wait where its mobs cannot see them (or follow you, when there is no such place
  or they do not get there), and the panel says so.
- A swelling creeper, the farm's or any other that comes up to them (at night too, and also while they fight another mob
  or fetch your things): one of the farm that one blow kills before it goes off they turn to and kill with that one
  blow. They count what a fall into the farm adds to its fuse, which only the server sees, so one that has just dropped
  into the pit is not theirs to strike. From any other they get out of its sight or its reach, take another place when it
  follows them or they cannot get there, and wait there until no creeper they cannot kill in time is near the spot they go
  back to; once no creeper near them has swelled for half a second, they fight a mob that attacks them meanwhile. When a
  creeper keeps them from the farm for two minutes, or drives them off three times, they leave the farm once it no longer
  swells at them: they wait where the farm's mobs cannot see them, or follow you when it was not one of the farm's own or
  there is no such place.
- A mob with a spear that reaches them: they go to another side, or away until it is gone.
- Hungry and low with nothing to eat: they give up, follow you again and fetch food.

**At night**, when someone sleeps or you lie in bed near them, they stop hitting (`penNightPause`) and wait where the
pen's mobs cannot see them, so that the server can count them idle and the night can pass. When there is a bed for them
they go to bed, and they farm on in the morning.

**The end:** crouch twice within six blocks of them; you need not look at them. They crouch once, step back to where the
pen's mobs cannot see them and wait there; in a base they keep the base. Look at them and crouch twice while they step
back, and they follow you at once. A tap, a mode, a job, AFK mode, stopping the following, or the switch `followMobFarm`
off end it too, and then they follow you again. The panel's **Stop farming** works like the sign.

**They give up by themselves**, and say why: when they die, are teleported, go to another dimension, get more than 32
blocks from the pen, fall into it, also while they step back from it (the panel asks you to get them out; from that
moment they fight the pen's mobs down there), when the pen is gone, when they cannot reach a spot, with no food, or when
AFK mode or another job of theirs keeps them from the farm for twenty seconds (a fight, a menu or driving them from the panel
only pauses it, and then they go on). After a restart of their game or the server they take the pen up again when they
stand at it, but never after a death.

**Guarding you at a pit**, a farm or not: a mob out of their reach in a closed space more than a block below the ground they
stand on that they would not climb or swim back out of, or in a farm's cell, they leave be, for the way to it is a fall
into it (the panel says so once). A dip one deep or a crater they climb out of in steps, wherever they stand, a water hole
they swim out of (also over mud or soul sand), a pit with a door or gate they open to ground beyond it, or a room behind a
door at their level they step into and fight in as ever; an iron door or a trapdoor is no way out for them; a cell under a
drop shaft is a farm's, door or not. A mob that climbs out of a pit and comes for you they fight at once. A mob on the
rim of the pen they farm, or a phantom over it, is theirs to fight too. When they stay somewhere and such a mob they leave be
hits them, they walk to the nearest place that no such mob near can see, also from the far side of its pit, and wait
there half a minute before they go on with what they were doing; where every place near is in its sight, they walk out of
its reach, more than 16 blocks from it and its pit. Hit there anyway, or unable to get there, they try another place,
three in all. With nowhere to go they follow you again; in a base they keep the base (unless they are down to three hearts).
The panel says so, and also when they are shot at in the base again and again.

The buddy kills hostile mobs only: no boss, warden, shulker, named mob or pet, and an enderman or a piglin only when it is
angry or when one blow kills it. The experience the mobs drop comes to the buddy as in vanilla. `penOwnerAwayMin` (0: they farm on
when you leave) ends the farm that many minutes after you went offline.

## Fetching an item

Order an item and a count (the panel's mode **Obtain items**, `!obtain` on Telegram, or `{"cmd":"obtain",...}`) and the
buddy works out every step to it from the game's own recipes and drops, and does them: from the base's chests when they
are near, or gathered, crafted and smelted (a better pickaxe first, a furnace, fuel), and asked again after every step,
so that a step that brought more or less than planned changes the rest. Following stops while they work on it; when it
is there they bring it to whoever ordered it, or keep it when that player is not online. [Obtain items](obtain.md) has how
they go about it.

Staying alive comes before the order:

- **a kit before a danger**: a crafting table, wood, a sword and a spare pickaxe before going underground; iron armour, a
  shield, food, torches, a water bucket and blocks before the deep; more for the nether and the End. All the kit lacks is
  fetched in one go;
- **the night in the ground** without iron armour (a thunderstorm counts as night): three blocks down under the open sky
  and shut, or shut in where they stand underground, out again in the morning; and the same, hurt under half health with
  no fight on, until healed;
- **walls against what shoots, and under the ground against all**: from skeletons and the like they go three down where
  the ground is good, or wall themselves in where they stand with blocks they carry, the shooters' side first, and stay
  until none is about; under the ground monsters on their way are walled out the same way, so no tunnel becomes a chase;
- **fight or flight by numbers**: a fight they would lose on this difficulty is run from in the open, never down a
  tunnel and never where the walker finds no way; a place where a patrol of pillagers was seen or something shot at them
  is kept away from for a while;
- **up by their own stair**: a step only the surface has (trees, the animals of the fields), taken deep under the ground,
  starts with a stair dug straight up to the sky, not a walk through the caves;
- **tools renewed in time**: a tool or armour piece in its last tenth counts as gone, so a new one is made while the old
  still works, and its last uses are spent only when nothing else breaks the block;
- **never over a drop a slip does not survive**: no bridge over more than three blocks of nothing, and crouched they step
  back onto the block that holds them before anything lets go of the crouch;
- **rather give up than risk dying**: an order with no safe way is given up, with its reason.

## How the buddy gets about

They plan their way themselves, cell by cell, and walk it with the real keys: level ground and slabs, a jump up, drops of
up to three blocks (deeper only into water), ladders and vines, doors and gates, water in three dimensions, and jumps
over gaps of up to five blocks, but only where a miss is survivable. They walk on while they still turn (a cell to the
side takes the strafe key). Where walking does not get them there, the buddy also:

- **digs** through one or two blocks of natural ground (earth, stone, sand, leaves) to go on at the same height, with
  the fastest tool they have, never a block with something inside it, one beside water or lava, or one with sand or
  gravel above it;
- **builds up** a block at a time, jumping and putting a block under their feet;
- **bridges** a gap, crouched at the edge with a block against the side of their own floor.

Those three cost much more than walking, so they only use them where there is no other way. What they put down is a
block they can spare (dirt, cobblestone and the like) and it stays. Within 64 blocks of a base's bed or 32 of a chest
they know they never dig (the blocks there are your build), but they do build up and bridge, so a step two up or a gap
in or by the base does not stop them. `pathDig` and `pathPlace` in the settings switch them off. Their plans keep away from lava, fire, magma, cactus, berry bushes, cobwebs and powder snow, and from
monsters, where there is a way round.

**When they get stuck** they get loose by themselves: twelve seconds without getting anywhere, half a minute of going to and
fro on the same few blocks, or two seconds held in a cobweb, powder snow or a berry bush, and they shuffle loose (a
random side, a step back, jumps), leave the cells they wandered over out of their plans for a minute and plan anew.
**What fails twice they leave alone**: a spot they could not reach, or a block they could not dig, twice within ten minutes,
is not tried again for ten minutes, and they say so once.

## How the buddy decides

Many things want the buddy's hands; one at a time gets them, in this order: staying alive (air under water, a fire, getting
loose, a creeper swelling at them at a farm), a menu they are busy in, a fight, an order to fetch an item, the corpse guard, your taps, farming mobs,
carrying things home, keeping the base, staying, sleeping, riding, working along, and following. The first that wants
the moment takes it, and whatever was going on carries on as soon as nothing above it wants it any more. Air under water
comes first also when they have nothing else to do. The panel shows
which one has their hands now, and their reports name it when it changes (`doing`).

From outside, the web panel goes before all of it: while you steer them or use their inventory there, nothing else moves
them or clicks in their inventory.

## What the buddy carries

The two best of each tool kind and of swords (by speed or damage, enchantments, durability left), the weapon they
fight with, one spare piece per armour slot besides what they wear, up to two stacks each of food, torches and
rockets, two totems, eight golden apples and four enchanted ones, their best bow and two stacks of arrows, saplings, a stack of each kind of seed, and their
shield, compass, clock, map, elytra and shears. When all their food is poor (two points a bite or less, beetroot) they
fetch better food from a chest that has some.
Everything else goes into your chests, or is thrown at your feet when no chest of yours is near.
